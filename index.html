Core Technology · Zero Power

We don't create energy. We reallocate it.

An ornamental fish tank already runs a 20W circulation motor. As a waterwheel grinds grain with falling water, LJR takes the potential energy those 20 watts have already created — and cleans, humidifies and deodorizes the room with no power of its own.

Core Technology · Zero Cleaning

No one scrubs it. The current and the roots do the work.

Still water grows moss; running water does not. Waste does settle, but a person never has to remove it. The dual-tank flow carries it out of the inner tank and the surplus leaves by overflow, descending the outer wall as a film. The roots of the vegetables and flowers above take up and purify the nitrogen, and seven filtration stages return clean water to the ornamental fish. All a person does is replace the filter box and top up the water that has evaporated.

Cleaning-free analysis

Carbon analysis

A stand of pines, in one table.
